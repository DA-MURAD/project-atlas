# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic business goals that Taleen Retail Group aims to achieve through the implementation of TaleenERP.

These goals serve as the foundation for all business capabilities, business processes, functional requirements, KPIs, dashboards, analytics, and AI models developed throughout Project Atlas.

---

# 2. Strategic Vision

Taleen Retail Group aims to become Saudi Arabia's leading omnichannel retailer by leveraging technology, operational excellence, and data-driven decision-making to deliver exceptional customer experiences and sustainable business growth.

---

# 3. Strategic Business Goals

## BG-001 — Revenue Growth

### Objective

Increase annual company revenue through expansion of physical stores, e-commerce, and improved sales performance.

### Success Indicators

- Revenue Growth %
- Sales Growth %
- Average Order Value
- Customer Lifetime Value

---

## BG-002 — Improve Customer Experience

### Objective

Provide a seamless shopping experience across all sales channels.

### Success Indicators

- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Average Resolution Time
- Customer Retention Rate

---

## BG-003 — Optimize Inventory

### Objective

Maintain optimal inventory levels while minimizing stock shortages and excess inventory.

### Success Indicators

- Inventory Turnover
- Stock Availability
- Stockout Rate
- Overstock Percentage

---

## BG-004 — Increase Operational Efficiency

### Objective

Reduce manual work through automation and standardized business processes.

### Success Indicators

- Process Cycle Time
- Employee Productivity
- Order Processing Time
- Warehouse Picking Time

---

## BG-005 — Strengthen Financial Performance

### Objective

Improve profitability while maintaining healthy cash flow.

### Success Indicators

- Gross Profit Margin
- Net Profit Margin
- Operating Cost Ratio
- Cash Conversion Cycle

---

## BG-006 — Improve Procurement Performance

### Objective

Develop reliable supplier relationships while reducing procurement costs.

### Success Indicators

- Supplier On-Time Delivery
- Procurement Lead Time
- Purchase Cost Savings
- Supplier Performance Score

---

## BG-007 — Build a Data-Driven Organization

### Objective

Enable executives to make strategic decisions using accurate and real-time business intelligence.

### Success Indicators

- Dashboard Adoption Rate
- Data Accuracy
- Report Availability
- Executive Decision Cycle Time

---

## BG-008 — Enable Artificial Intelligence

### Objective

Leverage machine learning and AI to improve forecasting, customer engagement, and operational decision-making.

### Success Indicators

- Forecast Accuracy
- Recommendation Accuracy
- Demand Forecast Accuracy
- AI Adoption Rate

---

# 4. Business Objectives

To achieve these strategic goals, Taleen Retail Group will focus on the following objectives.

- Increase annual sales revenue.
- Expand digital sales.
- Improve inventory utilization.
- Reduce operational expenses.
- Improve employee productivity.
- Improve customer retention.
- Improve supplier collaboration.
- Improve financial visibility.
- Increase forecasting accuracy.
- Support enterprise scalability.
