# 1. Executive Summary

Project Atlas is an enterprise software engineering initiative to design, develop, and document a complete Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) ecosystem from first principles.

Unlike traditional portfolio projects that focus on individual technologies, Project Atlas follows a business-first approach where every software component is derived from real business requirements and enterprise architecture principles.

The project simulates the operations of a modern omnichannel retail organization operating in Saudi Arabia, providing realistic business processes, transactional data, analytics, and artificial intelligence capabilities.

The platform will demonstrate the complete enterprise data lifecycle, including operational systems, data engineering, business intelligence, machine learning, and AI-powered decision support.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

To build an enterprise-grade ERP platform that serves as a reference implementation for modern software architecture, business intelligence, data engineering, and artificial intelligence.

---

# 3. Mission Statement

Design and implement a scalable ERP ecosystem that accurately models real-world retail operations while demonstrating enterprise software engineering best practices.

---

# 4. Business Background

Modern organizations generate massive volumes of operational data across multiple departments including sales, purchasing, inventory, finance, human resources, customer service, and logistics.

Many educational projects demonstrate isolated technologies such as SQL or Power BI. Very few demonstrate how these technologies integrate into a complete enterprise ecosystem.

Project Atlas addresses this gap by building an end-to-end enterprise platform that models real business operations and supports advanced analytics and AI-driven decision making.

---

# 5. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of Project Atlas are:

- Design a realistic enterprise ERP platform.
- Model real-world retail business operations.
- Build a normalized operational database.
- Generate realistic transactional data through business simulation.
- Develop enterprise ETL pipelines.
- Build a dimensional data warehouse.
- Create executive and operational Power BI dashboards.
- Develop machine learning solutions using enterprise data.
- Implement AI-powered business assistants.
- Document every architectural decision throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# 6. Business Goals

The ERP platform shall enable the organization to:

- Increase operational efficiency.
- Improve executive decision making.
- Reduce inventory shortages.
- Optimize inventory levels.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Support omnichannel sales.
- Improve employee productivity.
- Enable predictive analytics.
- Enable AI-assisted business decisions.
- Provide a scalable enterprise architecture.

# 7. Project Scope

The initial release includes the following business domains:

- Supplier Management

---

## Out of Scope (Initial Release)

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded from Version 1:

- Manufacturing
- Payroll Processing
- Advanced Tax Compliance
- International Operations
- Multi-language Support
- Mobile Applications
- Cloud-native Microservices

These may be introduced in future releases.

# 9. Guiding Principles

Project Atlas follows these architectural principles:

1. Business before Technology.
2. Every feature must solve a business problem.
3. Every database object must support a business capability.
4. Every report must support a business decision.
5. Every AI model must solve a measurable business problem.
6. Documentation is treated as a software deliverable.
7. Simplicity before complexity.
8. Scalability by design.
9. Security by design.
10. Data quality is non-negotiable.

---

# 14. Risks

Potential project risks include:

- Scope expansion
- Increased architectural complexity
- Large data generation requirements
- Performance optimization challenges
- Long project duration

Risk mitigation strategies will be documented throughout the project.

---

# 15. Assumptions

The project assumes:

- SQL Server is the primary operational database.
- Python is the primary programming language.
- Power BI is the reporting platform.
- Business processes are modeled after enterprise retail best practices.
- Simulated business data will follow realistic operational rules.
