# 2. Company Description

Taleen Retail Group (TRG) is a leading omnichannel retail organization specializing in consumer electronics, home appliances, and furniture.

The company operates both physical retail stores and digital commerce platforms across Saudi Arabia, providing customers with a seamless shopping experience through multiple sales channels.

TRG focuses on operational excellence, customer satisfaction, data-driven decision making, and digital transformation.
